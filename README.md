# RoleColorAI — Project Overview

## Summary

RoleColorAI is the artificial intelligence engine powering **RoleColorFinder**, designed to reconstruct a person's resume through the lens of their **RoleColor profile**. Instead of producing a standard CV, it highlights what makes individuals distinct within teams — Builders (innovation, leadership, strategy), Enablers (collaboration, execution, bridge roles), Thrivers (adaptability, results under pressure), and Supportees (reliability, consistency, dependability). This approach benefits both candidates and employers by showcasing natural strengths and improving team-role fit, while also expanding RoleColorFinder's scope into HR, recruiting, and career services.

## Mission Statement

**To create the world's first AI that rewrites resumes through a team-role lens**, bridging personal career growth with RoleColorFinder's broader mission of enabling alignment within organizations.

## Core Features

The system's core features include:

- **Resume Parsing**: Extracting clean text from uploaded files
- **RoleColor Alignment**: Mapping content to color traits
- **Resume Rewriting**: Emphasizing RoleColor-based strengths and ATS optimization
- **Output Formatting**: PDF/DOCX exports
- **Fit Scoring** *(Future Feature)*: Match candidates' resumes to job descriptions and team RoleColor profiles
